Animal Kingdom Support

Email sidus.network+support@gmail.com with your device model, operating-system and app version, game mode or stage, and steps to reproduce the issue. If attaching screenshots, remove personal information first.

For gameplay-record deletion, include the participant code at the bottom of Settings. Resetting progress or uninstalling does not delete server records. Do not send passwords, identity documents or payment details.

Daily reminders can be disabled in the app. Device notification permissions may also need adjustment. Progress is currently stored locally; account-based recovery and cross-device synchronization are not available. We review requests but may not respond immediately.
